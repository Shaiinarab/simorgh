# Adoption specs

Port specs: what to take from each working open-source system, and what to refuse. Each one names the task that would implement it.

* [GhostBrain → Simorgh adoption spec](GhostBrain-ADOPTION.md) — What to take from GhostBrain's memory model and what to refuse.
* [Port spec — api-access-gateway → Simorgh](api-access-gateway-ADOPTION.md) — What to take from api-access-gateway's key-handling model and where it duplicates our probes.
* [Hermes-stack → Simorgh adoption spec](hermes-stack-ADOPTION.md) — Row 11 of the reference cross-walk: adopted-with-changes by ADR-0008.
* [OmniRouter → Simorgh adoption spec](omnirouter-ADOPTION.md) — What to take from OmniRouter's provider catalogue shape and what stays refused.
* [REA MCP contract-generation — adoption for Simorgh](rea-mcp-contracts-ADOPTION.md) — What to take from generated MCP contracts, and the open question about platformMcpHandler auth.
